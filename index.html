Questions, answered clearly

Before you enrol.

Are you an official CELPIP test centre?

No. PrimeScore is an independent preparation provider. Official test registration and score decisions are handled by CELPIP/Paragon Testing Enterprises.

Can you guarantee a particular score?

No ethical provider can guarantee a test score. We provide focused teaching, feedback and strategy; your result depends on your current English level, preparation and performance on test day.

Can you register for me?

We can guide you step by step, but you should create and control your own official account, confirm your personal information and complete payment through the official registration system.

Can you get my score upgraded?

We cannot promise or decide an upgrade. We can review your concern, explain whether an official re-evaluation may be worth considering and help you present a clear request. CELPIP makes the final decision.

Are classes online or in person?

Online preparation is available. Add your confirmed in-person location here when it is ready.
